# Where Plastic Memories still gets talked about

If you want to share this project somewhere people might actually see it,
or just want proof this isn't being built in a vacuum — here's what turned
up searching. Confidence level noted for each; nobody has actually gone and
lurked in these to confirm current activity beyond what's visible from
outside.

## Confirmed active

- **[MyAnimeList — anime page](https://myanimelist.net/anime/27775)** and
  its forum. Over a million members on the page, and still getting new
  reviews as of March 2026 — more than a decade after it aired. Multiple
  "please make a season 2" forum threads spanning 2013 through 2024 (see
  `research/confirmed/anime-ending.md` for specifics and links). Probably
  the single best place to post this if you want it seen by people who
  already love the source material.
- **r/PlaMemo — Isla's Paradise** (Discord, ~1,580 members) — general fan
  hangout for the anime.
- **#plamemo** on X/Twitter — the show's own hashtag, per its LiveChart
  listing.

## Niche, but exactly on-topic

- **Plastic Memories (VN) Việt hóa** (Discord, ~90 members) — a
  Vietnamese-language visual novel translation community. Small, but the
  only community found that's organized specifically around the VN, which
  is the branch this whole project leans on hardest.

## Evidence people actually want this

The [Change.org petition](https://www.change.org/p/alternative-ending-happy-ending-or-remake-version-for-plastic-memories-series)
covered in `research/confirmed/anime-ending.md` — a real, if small, ask for
exactly this kind of alternate ending.

## What wasn't found

No dedicated, currently-active subreddit turned up in search. If you know
of one (or a Discord/forum missing from this list), add it — that's a
one-line PR.
