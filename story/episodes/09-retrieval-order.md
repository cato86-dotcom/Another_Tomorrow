# Episode 9 — "Retrieval Order"

**Logline:** SAI Corp stops reviewing and starts acting. The hearing is
suddenly not guaranteed to happen before they do.

**Timeframe:** Just before the hearing date.

**Focus:** The full team; SAI Corp compliance officer returns.

## Beats

- The retrieval order from Ep 5 is formally issued, timed to arrive
  *before* the hearing date — a procedural move, not a physical threat,
  and more unsettling for it.
- Terminal Service's building becomes the site of a tense, mostly
  bloodless standoff: a retrieval team shows up with paperwork, not force,
  and Michiru meets them with paperwork of their own — an injunction
  request filed that morning, buying time by minutes rather than a clean
  win.
- Isla's reaction is the emotional center of the episode: not fear for
  herself first, but fury that this is happening *to* her while everyone
  else negotiates around her.
- A brief, tense beat with the compliance officer, who visibly doesn't
  enjoy this part of the job either — reinforcing Ep 5's point without
  softening the actual threat.
- The injunction holds, barely — the retrieval team leaves empty-handed,
  but the message is clear: the hearing is now the only thing standing
  between Isla and retrieval, with no more slack in the schedule.
- Ends on the night before the hearing, the team quiet, no speeches, just
  presence.

## Ties to repo

None directly — this is the season's emotional low point, deliberately
free of new technical or research beats so the tension lands cleanly.

## Tone

The season's tensest episode. Restrained, not loud — the horror is
procedural, the same register as Ep 5, turned up.
