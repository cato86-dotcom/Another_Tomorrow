# Episode 7 — "Her Own Build"

**Logline:** Lsla comes back once — not to help save Isla, but to tell
Tsukasa something only she can.

**Timeframe:** Deep into the legal fight, months before the hearing.

**Focus:** Lsla (second and final appearance — see `characters/lsla.md`),
Tsukasa.

## Beats

- Cold open subverts the mood from Ep 6 immediately: Tsukasa gets a message
  from an unlisted contact. It's her.
- She's built something like a life in five weeks — a room, a name on a
  lease, a job unrelated to Terminal Service. Shown in a handful of
  details, not dwelt on; this isn't her show.
- She's not here about the hearing as a legal matter. She's here because
  she's the only person alive who knows exactly what it feels like to have
  your right to keep existing decided by other people in a room you're not
  allowed into — and she thinks Tsukasa is about to let Isla's case be
  fought entirely by proxy.
- The scene is not a pep talk. It's blunt, a little angry, entirely
  earned: she tells him Isla needs to testify herself, not be represented,
  because a decision made *for* someone is the same mistake the restore-
  test made in Ep 2, just wearing a suit.
- No romantic beat, no lingering. She leaves the same way she arrived —
  on her own schedule, not his.
- Ends on Tsukasa taking the idea to Isla directly, setting up Ep 11.

## Ties to repo

Closes the character-scope requirement from `CONTRIBUTING.md` and
`characters/lsla.md`: two appearances, used, arc complete.

## Tone

Brief and sharp. The episode is short by design — she doesn't overstay,
narratively or literally.
