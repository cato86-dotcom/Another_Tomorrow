# Episode 4 — "Warm Boot"

**Logline:** She wakes up. That's the whole episode, and it's enough.

**Timeframe:** Week 3, days later.

**Focus:** Isla, Tsukasa.

## Beats

- Deliberately small in scale after Ep 3's tension. No press conference, no
  crowd — just the room, Eru monitoring quietly from a console in the
  corner, Moegi watching from the doorway rather than inserting herself
  into the moment.
- Isla wakes disoriented but intact — memory checks confirmed *before* she
  opens her eyes, so the audience already knows what she doesn't yet: it
  worked. The scene isn't "will it work," it's "watching her find out."
- First thing she asks about isn't the repair. It's how long she was
  under, and whether Tsukasa kept the promise from Ep 1 to not let it
  happen to her again mid-shift.
- No dialogue about Lsla yet — deliberately withheld. That conversation
  belongs to a later, quieter episode, not this one.
- The rest of the episode is small and unhurried: her first meal back,
  Michiru and Kazuki visiting separately rather than as a group scene,
  Zack saying something inappropriate and Isla laughing at it, which is
  itself the point — she's allowed to just be a person having an ordinary
  good day.
- Ends on Tsukasa starting to say something about the future and Isla
  cutting him off, gently: not yet, she says. Let this one day just be a
  day. (Sets up why Ep 12's "next weekend" beat lands as growth, not
  repetition.)

## Ties to repo

None directly — this episode is the reward for `experiments/tuned-v1`
working, not a demonstration of it.

## Tone

Warm, unhurried, almost uneventful on purpose. The season's other
emotional peak (Ep 11) earns its intensity partly because this one didn't
reach for it.
