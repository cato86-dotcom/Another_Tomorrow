# Episode 6 — "A Normal Tuesday"

**Logline:** A completely ordinary Giftia retrieval job, while a much
bigger deadline no one mentions out loud sits in the background of every
scene.

**Timeframe:** Week 4, a few days after Ep 5.

**Focus:** The full Terminal Service team as an ensemble — deliberately
not Tsukasa/Isla-centric this time.

## Beats

- Standalone case-of-the-week structure, unrelated to the season arc on
  its surface: an elderly client's Giftia companion is nearing a natural,
  unforced retrieval, handled with the same tenderness the original series
  brought to these episodes.
- Isla works the case herself — first field assignment since Ep 4 — and
  the episode lets her be competent and a little rusty at once, rather
  than either fully recovered or fragile.
- Kazuki and Zack get a genuinely funny B-plot (candidate: a logistics
  mixup with the retrieval paperwork that almost mirrors Ep 5's tone
  without commenting on it directly).
- Michiru has one quiet scene with Isla, not about the SAI Corp situation
  at all — just two colleagues talking, which does more to sell their
  friendship than a dedicated "support Isla" scene would.
- The episode never mentions the pending retrieval order. That's the
  point — normal life continuing under threat, not paused by it.
- Ends on the team packing up after a good day's work, ordinary and warm,
  right before the cold open of Ep 7 breaks that mood.

## Ties to repo

None — intentionally. Not every episode needs to serve the technical or
legal plot; this one exists to protect the show's sense of humor.

## Tone

Light. The season's one clean breather before the back half tightens.
