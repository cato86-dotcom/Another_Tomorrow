# Episode 3 — "Wear Leveling"

**Logline:** The repair almost works the first time. Almost isn't good
enough, and the team has to say so out loud.

**Timeframe:** The long middle stretch — several months of research, most of it tedious on purpose.

**Focus:** Moegi, Eru, Tsukasa. Isla is present only as a silent chamber in
the background of every scene.

## Beats

- Montage energy, but grounded: late nights, a whiteboard that keeps
  getting erased and redrawn, Eru running numbers that don't cooperate.
- First attempt at the repair looks promising on paper and fails in
  practice — the in-story version of `experiments/baseline-v0`'s honest
  "didn't fully work yet" result. Play the failure straight, not as a fake-
  out before an easy win.
- Tsukasa wants to ship the first version anyway, because waiting feels
  unbearable. Moegi refuses, and the refusal is framed as care, not
  obstruction — a callback to her Ep 1 condition.
- Eru finds the actual lever almost by accident, buried in a parameter
  nobody was watching closely: not more raw capacity, but a change to how
  memories get batched together before they're written at all. A small
  found detail lands bigger than a dramatic breakthrough would.
- The team re-runs the numbers across many more trial conditions this
  time, not just one — deliberately shown as tedious, not a montage-ending
  eureka. The payoff is confidence, not spectacle.
- Ends on Moegi finally saying the words "I think this works" — flatly,
  exhausted, unsentimental. Not a speech.

## Ties to repo

Structurally mirrors `experiments/tuned-v1/notes.md`: the fix that works
isn't the first idea, it's the one that got tested enough times to trust.

## Tone

Process, not magic. The episode's tension comes from rigor, not danger.
