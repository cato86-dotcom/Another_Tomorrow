# Episode 12 — "Next Weekend"

**Logline:** Nine months after Day 0 — the same gap canon gave Tsukasa to
grieve Isla and meet a new partner. This time, the office door opens on a
different face.

**Timeframe:** Nine months after Day 0 (see `SEASON-BIBLE.md`'s "Connecting
to the anime's own ending"). The same mark canon already used.

**Focus:** Isla, Tsukasa, full ensemble in a lighter capacity.

## Beats

- Cold open deliberately mirrors the anime's own real epilogue, shot for
  shot where possible: Tsukasa walking into the Terminal Service office,
  visibly steadier than the pilot-episode version of himself, about to be
  introduced to his "new partner." Anyone who's seen the original ending
  will recognize the staging before they understand why.
- The show holds the reveal for one extra beat longer than expected — then
  turns, and it's Isla. Not a twist sting, no swelling music cue; played
  as something the audience half-expected the moment they clocked the
  staging. The point isn't surprise. It's recognition.
- From there, this episode picks up right where Ep 11 left off emotionally:
  the reaction that episode withheld. Not a party — just the team,
  together, quiet relief more than celebration at first.
- A montage of ordinary time passing, deliberately unhurried: Isla back on
  the job, Terminal Service business as usual, small callbacks to Ep 6's
  case-of-the-week energy — the show returning to its own normal register
  on purpose.
- One scene addresses the thing the season refuses to pretend it solved:
  Isla and Tsukasa, alone, talking honestly about the fact that nothing
  was made permanent — Ep 11's testimony wasn't a rhetorical flourish, it
  was true, and they both have to actually live inside that truth now, not
  just have won an argument about it.
- The ferris wheel returns — same location as the canon ending, deliberate
  callback — but the beat is different: instead of a goodbye neither of
  them chose, it's Tsukasa asking what she wants to do *next weekend*, and
  meaning it as an ordinary question with an ordinary answer, not a vow.
- Isla's answer is small and specific — a plan, not a promise. That
  specificity is the point: this is what "acceptance criteria: she gets to
  keep them" (Issue #1) actually looks like on screen.
- Ends on the ferris wheel turning, the two of them making plans, no
  swelling declaration that this is forever — just two people who get to
  find out.

## Ties to repo

Directly closes Issue #1 from `README.md`; completes the design note in

ordinary future chosen anyway; and resolves the canon connection laid out

## Tone

Warm, unhurried, quietly happy — the tone `SEASON-BIBLE.md`'s "what the
finale must not do" section is written to protect. The cold open is the one
moment this episode allows itself real weight before settling into that
warmth.
