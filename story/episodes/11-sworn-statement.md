# Episode 11 — "Sworn Statement"

**Logline:** Everyone else has argued about Isla for two episodes. Now she
gets to speak.

**Timeframe:** Hearing, day 2.

**Focus:** Isla, Tsukasa, the panel.

## Beats

- Isla testifies, following through on the choice Ep 7 and Ep 8 set up —
  not represented, not spoken for. Her opening line reframes the entire
  hearing: she isn't asking to be declared cured. She's asking to be
  treated as someone whose future is hers to risk.
- SAI Corp's counsel cross-examines her directly, and the episode doesn't
  soften it — including a version of the buffer-tail limitation raised in
  Ep 10, put to her personally: *there's no guarantee this holds forever.
  Doesn't that concern you?* Her answer is the season's thesis, delivered
  plainly, not as a speech: nothing holds forever for anyone. That was
  never the question.
- A brief, devastating beat where counsel invokes Lsla — is it right that
  another person exists because of what was done trying to save her? Isla
  doesn't deflect it. She answers honestly, including the part that isn't
  comfortable, without letting it become the reason to stop her case.
- Tsukasa is present but doesn't speak for her at any point in this
  episode — a deliberate structural choice, letting Ep 4's restraint pay
  off here.
- The panel's decision lands quietly, almost anticlimactically after the
  intensity of the testimony — not a dramatic gavel moment, closer to a
  procedural notice being read aloud. In Isla's favor.
- Ends the instant the decision is read — no reaction shots yet. That's
  Ep 12's job.

## Tone

The season's emotional peak. Spare, controlled — the intensity comes from
restraint, not volume.
