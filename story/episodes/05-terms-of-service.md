# Episode 5 — "Terms of Service"

**Logline:** SAI Corp doesn't call it a miracle. They call it a breach of
contract.

**Timeframe:** Not long after Isla wakes.

**Focus:** SAI Corp compliance officer (name open — see `SEASON-BIBLE.md`),
Tsukasa, Michiru.

## Beats

- A formal letter, not a raid. That's the tone from the start: procedural,
  polite, devastating. "Unauthorized modification of a Giftia's core
  systems voids retrieval warranty and constitutes a licensing violation
  under Section [X]" — read aloud in a flat corporate register.
- The compliance officer is introduced as competent and reasonable, not
  cruel — they're not wrong about what the contract says, which is worse
  than if they were. They're doing their job exactly correctly.
- Tsukasa's instinct is to get angry at a person. Michiru redirects him:
  the problem isn't a person, it's the terms themselves — which means the
  fight has to be about changing what's allowed, not about beating one
  official.
- The episode names the shape of the actual threat plainly: SAI Corp isn't
  claiming Isla is broken. They're claiming the *fix* is the crime.
- Terminal Service, as a workplace, quietly starts absorbing risk on
  Tsukasa's behalf — Michiru reassigning paperwork, Kazuki running
  interference with a supervisor — before anyone frames it as a team
  decision out loud.
- Ends on the compliance officer's closing line, delivered without malice:
  a retrieval order is "under review," not yet issued. A countdown starts
  that isn't given a number.

## Ties to repo

This episode is the dramatized version of this project's own origin joke —
the `chage -E -1` / Windows-activation bit from early in the project's own
history, now played straight instead of for laughs.

## Tone

Cold and procedural on the surface, with the fear underneath it doing all
the actual work. No shouting matches — SAI Corp doesn't need to raise its
voice to be frightening.
