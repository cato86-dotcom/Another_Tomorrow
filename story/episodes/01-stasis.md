# Episode 1 — "Stasis"

**Logline:** Before the wall hits, not after — Isla and Tsukasa choose
hibernation while there's still something left to save.

**Timeframe:** Day 0 — the day Isla was due to reach 81,920h.

**Focus:** Isla, Tsukasa, Moegi.

## Beats

- Cold open: an ordinary Terminal Service retrieval, intercut with the
  first sign something's off with Isla — nothing dramatic, just a half-
  second of lag that Eru's diagnostics flag and Isla brushes off.
- Eru's numbers say the wall is closer than the official estimate. The
  official 81,920h figure gets named on-screen for the first time as what
  it actually is: a conservative company floor, not Isla's real limit —
  planting the idea without explaining it yet.
- Tsukasa refuses the canon ending's premise out loud: he's not interested
  in "make good memories before the end," he wants an actual fix, and says
  so to Moegi directly.
- Moegi agrees to try, with a condition: it has to be done in stasis,
  in-place, on the original hardware. No copies, ever, for any reason. She
  states this as a hard rule before Tsukasa fully understands why it
  matters — he'll learn why in Ep 2.
- Isla's one condition, delivered quietly: she wants to choose the moment
  she goes under, not have it happen to her mid-shift like it almost just
  did.
- Ends on the stasis chamber closing. No swell of music promising it'll
  work — just Tsukasa alone in the room afterward.

## Ties to repo

`spec/ALMA-HYPOTHESIS.md`'s framing of the 81,920h figure as a possible
conservative estimate rather than a hard physical limit.

## Tone

Quiet, deliberate. This episode earns its calm — everything after gets
louder.
